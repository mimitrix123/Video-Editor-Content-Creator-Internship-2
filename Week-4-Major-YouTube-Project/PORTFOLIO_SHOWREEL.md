# Professional Video Editing Portfolio / Showreel

## Portfolio structure
### 1. Hero
`Video Editor & Content Creator`  
One-line value proposition: `Short-form, long-form, social-first edits, and creator workflows.`

### 2. Featured work
- Week 1 — Day in My Life Vlog
- Week 2 — Product Review / Tutorial
- Week 3 — Shorts & Reels Package
- Week 4 — YouTube Major Project

For each project show: objective, role, tools, editing techniques, final format, and a representative frame/link.

### 3. Showreel structure — 45–60 seconds
- 0:00–0:04: name/title card
- 0:04–0:15: strongest long-form moments
- 0:15–0:28: short-form cuts, captions, zooms, speed ramps
- 0:28–0:40: product/tutorial work, transitions, graphics
- 0:40–0:52: before/after or pacing examples
- 0:52–1:00: contact/CTA card

### 4. Skills section
- Premiere Pro / DaVinci Resolve / CapCut workflows as applicable
- Story structure and pacing
- Captions and motion graphics
- Color correction
- Audio cleanup and mixing
- YouTube/Shorts formatting
- Thumbnail design
- Basic SEO metadata

### 5. Contact
Add the user's actual email/social/portfolio URLs before publishing. Do not place private contact information in a public repository unless intended for publication.

## Portfolio quality checklist
- Consistent naming and thumbnails
- No unlicensed footage/music
- Show the editing decisions, not only final clips
- Keep the showreel concise
- Add measurable outcomes only when they are verified
