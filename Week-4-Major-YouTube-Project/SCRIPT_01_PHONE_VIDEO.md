# Video 01 — How to Shoot Better Videos With Your Phone

**Target duration:** ~5 minutes

## 0:00–0:20 — Hook
If your phone videos look flat, shaky, or dark, you probably don't need a new camera. You need a few simple changes to how you shoot. In the next five minutes, I'll show you a repeatable setup for cleaner, more professional-looking phone footage.

## 0:20–1:05 — Clean the lens and control the frame
Start with the easiest fix: clean the camera lens. Fingerprints reduce contrast and make highlights bloom. Next, choose your main camera rather than digitally zooming whenever possible. Keep your subject away from the extreme edge of the frame, and leave intentional space in the direction they're looking.

## 1:05–2:00 — Use better light
Put the main light in front of your subject. A window is an excellent free source. Turn your subject slightly toward it instead of placing the window directly behind them. If the background is much brighter than the face, move the subject or change your angle until the exposure looks balanced.

## 2:00–2:50 — Stabilize the shot
For talking-head footage, place the phone on a stable surface or tripod. For movement, hold the phone with both hands and keep your elbows close to your body. Record a few seconds before and after the action so editing has room for clean cuts.

## 2:50–3:45 — Compose and record clean audio
Keep the camera around eye level for a natural talking-head angle. Check the background for distractions. For audio, move the microphone closer to the speaker whenever possible. Record a short test and listen with headphones before filming the entire sequence.

## 3:45–4:35 — Record with editing in mind
Capture a wide establishing shot, a medium shot, and close-up detail shots. Hold each shot long enough to make a clean edit. Also record room tone for several seconds. These small choices give you options when building a five-minute video or cutting Shorts later.

## 4:35–5:00 — Recap and CTA
So the workflow is simple: clean the lens, find soft front light, stabilize the phone, check composition, and capture clean audio. Then record a mix of wide, medium, and detail shots. Save this video as a checklist, and subscribe for more practical creator workflows.
