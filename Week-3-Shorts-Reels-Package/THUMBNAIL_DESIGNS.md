# Week 3 — Matching Thumbnail Designs

Create three 1080×1920 master frames and export a 1280×720 landscape thumbnail crop where required by the publishing platform.

## Shared visual system
- Large bold sans-serif headline
- One dominant subject/object
- Strong foreground/background separation
- Same type scale, margins, and accent treatment across all three
- Keep important text inside a central safe zone
- Minimal copy: 3–6 words

## Thumbnail 01 — Productivity
**Headline:** `3 PRODUCTIVITY TIPS`  
**Secondary:** `STOP WASTING TIME`  
**Visual:** focused desk/work scene with a timer or checklist motif.  
**Composition:** subject on one side, headline on the opposite side.

## Thumbnail 02 — Camera Hacks
**Headline:** `PHONE CAMERA HACKS`  
**Secondary:** `TRY THESE 3`  
**Visual:** smartphone camera close-up with a framing guide/record indicator.  
**Composition:** phone large in foreground; headline in open negative space.

## Thumbnail 03 — Desk Setup
**Headline:** `DESK RESET`  
**Secondary:** `BEFORE → AFTER`  
**Visual:** split-screen messy vs organized desk.  
**Composition:** vertical split with a clear transformation cue.

## Export checklist
- High-resolution PNG/JPG
- Check readability at small size
- Verify no important text is cropped
- Maintain the same branding across all three thumbnails
