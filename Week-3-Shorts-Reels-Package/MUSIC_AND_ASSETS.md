# Music & Asset Guide

## Music
Use music that is currently licensed/cleared for the account and platform where the short will be published. For a real submission, record the track title, creator, source URL, license/usage terms, and download date in this file.

The demo project should use a placeholder/generated audio bed rather than claiming a commercial track is licensed.

## Footage
Replace placeholder scenes with:
- Your own recorded vertical clips, or
- Stock footage whose license explicitly permits the intended social-media use.

For every external asset, document source, creator, license, and any required attribution.

## SFX
Use cleared whoosh, click, pop, riser, and impact effects. Keep SFX short and below narration/music where appropriate.

## Publishing checks
- Confirm music rights for the destination platform/account.
- Confirm stock footage license covers social/commercial use if applicable.
- Keep captions readable above platform UI areas.
- Export at 1080×1920, 9:16, H.264/AAC.
