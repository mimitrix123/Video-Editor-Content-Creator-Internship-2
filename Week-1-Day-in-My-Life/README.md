# 🚀 Week 1 Mini Project — Day in My Life Vlog

A 2-minute **Day in My Life** vlog created for the Video Editor & Content Creator Internship — Week 1.

## 🎬 Deliverable

- **Format:** MP4 (H.264 + AAC)
- **Resolution:** 1920 × 1080 (1080p)
- **Frame rate:** 30 fps
- **Duration:** 2:01
- **Title card:** Included
- **Cuts:** 6 structured day-in-the-life sections
- **Transitions:** Fade, slide-left, fade-to-black, slide-right
- **Text overlays:** Time-of-day, scene labels, supporting captions
- **Background music:** Original generated ambient tone, mixed under the visuals

## Editing techniques demonstrated

1. **Cuts** — six distinct scenes create a clear vlog rhythm.
2. **Transitions** — short 1-second transitions keep scene changes smooth without overusing effects.
3. **Text overlays** — readable hierarchy for time, scene name, and supporting copy.
4. **Title card** — opening title establishes the vlog immediately.
5. **Background music** — low-volume ambient bed supports the sequence.
6. **1080p export** — final delivery is 1920×1080 at 30 fps.

## Note about footage

No personal footage was supplied with the assignment. To keep the submission self-contained, the final demo uses **original generated motion-graphic scenes** rather than copyrighted stock footage. The edit structure is intentionally ready for replacement with personal clips or properly licensed stock footage while preserving the cuts, transitions, overlays, music mix, and 1080p export settings.
